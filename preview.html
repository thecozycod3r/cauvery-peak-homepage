III Process

Grower to connoisseur

Nine stages.
One boundary.

  1. 05

    Drying

    Sun-dried on concrete terraces, turned by hand, down to under 12% moisture.

  2. 08

    Roasting

    Roasted after your order is placed, in small batches, on the estate. Never before, never to sit in a warehouse.

    Plate to come

    We have not put someone else's roastery here.

  3. 09

    Packed & shipped

    Rested 24 hours to cool, packed, and sent from the estate itself.
