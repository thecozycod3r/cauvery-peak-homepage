Cafés/Lake View Village Café

Lake View Village Café

A modern coffee experience on Yercaud Main Road. The interior is spacious, parking is ample, and it is the natural place to break a drive with friends or family.

Alongside the full range of estate coffee drinks there are local snacks and pastries chosen to go with them, and a retail corner with freshly roasted beans, merchandise and coffee accessories to take home.

  • Estate coffee drinks, snacks and local delicacies
  • Retail corner: beans, merchandise, accessories
  • Ample parking
  • Spacious indoor seating
  • Views over the surrounding hills
