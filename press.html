MSP Plantations · Yercaud, Tamil Nadu

In print

The Shevaroys have been on record since 1922.

This is not a new estate that discovered marketing. The hills were named in the standard reference work on coffee a century ago, and the family has been farming them for five generations.

All About Coffee

William H. Ukers

1922

The standard reference work on coffee names the Shevaroy Hills as a source of the best Indian coffee. A century on, the estate is still farming the same hills.

Home Coffee Roasting

Kenneth Davids

1996

Kenneth Davids — author, coffee expert and co-founder of Coffee Review — wrote about Cauvery Peak's coffee. One of the few Indian estates to be named in his writing.

Indian Coffee Magazine

Grower to Connoisseur

August 2017

The trade press covered the launch of Cauvery Peak as a direct-to-drinker brand, and the estate's decision to roast and ship its own crop.
