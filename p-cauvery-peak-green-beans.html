Cauvery Peak Green Beans

This immaculately manicured plantation, at 1400mts/ 4600ft, is unique in India for the Hawaiian Red Catturra (HRC) strain of Arabica coffee which helps produce a truly well balanced cup of coffee. MSP Plantations is a family owned, professionally managed company.
