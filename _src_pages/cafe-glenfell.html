Glenfell Estate Kiosk

The kiosk sits at the 17th hairpin bend on the Salem–Yercaud Ghat Road — for many travellers a fixed stop on the way up or down, as much for the view as the coffee.

Freshly brewed coffee to drink by the road, and packaged beans and ground coffee to take home or give away. It is named for Glenfell, the lowest and warmest of the three estates, where the coffee is inter-planted with cloves, nutmeg and pepper — taste it here.

  • Freshly brewed coffee to go
  • Packaged beans and ground coffee
  • Panoramic views down the valley
  • A well-known photo stop
  • A place to rest halfway up the hill
