Cauvery Peak

Kenneth Davids - Coffee expert, author and co founder of Coffee Review rated Cauvery Peak coffee among the top 3 Indian Arabica coffees. This immaculately manicured plantation, at 1400mts/ 4600ft, is unique in India for the Hawaiian Red Catturra (HRC) strain of Arabica coffee which helps produce a truly well balanced cup of coffee.
