1867 to today

A legacy built over 150 years.

Four names, five generations, and three estates in the Shevaroy Hills. The coffee growing here today comes from seed carried back from Costa Rica in 1965.

  1. 1867

    Cauvery Peak estate is assigned to Mr E. Joachim by British India.

  2. 1925

    Rao Bahadur Senthikumar Nadar, a merchant from Virudhunagar, acquires his first estate at Devikolam in Kerala — coffee and spices.

  3. 1936

    MSP Nadar travels to Europe to export raw coffee under the name Hill Tiller & Co.

  4. 1937

    MSP Nadar purchases Glenfell Estate.

  5. 1942

    MSP Nadar purchases Thangavilla Bungalow.

  6. 1957

    Mrs Thangama Nadar and Mr MSP Rajes purchase Cauvery Peak Estate, and form MSP Nadar Sons to administer the estates.

  7. 1965

    MSP Rajes travels to Costa Rica and brings back Hawaiian Red Caturra seed — the variety the estate still grows.

  8. 1975

    MSP Plantations is formed by Mr MSP Rajes and Mr Vijayan Rajes, in place of MSP Nadar Sons.

  9. 1985

    Vijayan Rajes begins managing MSP Plantations and the estates.

  10. 1987

    Shevaroys estate is purchased by Mr Vijayan Rajes.

  11. 2016

    Vishnu Rajes joins the MSP Plantations management team.

  12. 2017

    Cauvery Peak is launched as a brand by Mr Vijayan Rajes and Mr Vishnu Rajes.

The people who carried it forward

Two generations, currently running it.

Vijayan Rajes

Chairman · managing the estates since 1985

Son of MSP Rajes. He studied business management (finance) at San Diego State University and took an MBA at the Thunderbird School of Global Management, Arizona, then came home and found farming was his real passion. After working in the family’s green-coffee export business from 1985 to 1996, he turned to modernising the plantations.

  • Vice Chairman, Coffee Board of India, 1993 — responsible for introducing free marketing of coffee by growers
  • President, Planters’ Association of Tamil Nadu, 2004–06
  • President, UPASI (United Planters’ Association of Southern India), 2014–15
  • Chairman, Shevaroy Planters’ Association, and President of the Yercaud Club since 1996

Vishnu Rajes

Marketing and customer relations · joined 2016

Son of Vijayan Rajes. He studied business management at Washington College in the US on an academic scholarship, graduating in 2015, and interned at Atlantic (USA), a commodity trading house in New York, and at AG Acquisitions in Bangalore. Back home he took the Coffee Board-supported programme in coffee entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Plantation Management, Bangalore, joined the management team in 2016 and launched Cauvery Peak with his father in 2017.

  • Manages marketing and customer relations for Cauvery Peak
  • Captained his college’s NCAA Division III tennis team, and was ranked in the All India Tennis Association’s top 100
