Return and Refund Policy

The terms below are the ones that apply to your order. They are the same policies that govern checkout on the estate store.

At Cauvery Peak Estate, we are committed to ensuring your complete satisfaction with our products. If you are not entirely happy with your purchase, we’re here to help.

Eligibility for Returns

• Returns must be initiated within 7 days of receiving your order.

• The product must be unused, unopened, and in its original packaging.

• Products purchased as part of a set or promotional offer must be returned as a whole set.

Non-Returnable Items

• Items purchased on final sale or during promotional events.

• Gift cards or e-vouchers.

• Products that are opened, used, or not in their original condition.

How to Initiate a Return

1. Email us at cauverypeakestateorders@gmail.com with your order number and reason for the return.

2. Our team will provide you with a return authorization number and further instructions.

3. Ship the product back to the address provided. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

Refund Process

• Once we receive and inspect the returned product, we will notify you of the status of your refund.

• Refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 7-10 business days.

Damaged or Defective Products

• If your product arrives damaged or defective, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue.

• We will arrange for a replacement or a full refund.

Contact Us

For any queries or concerns, please reach out to our customer service team at cauverypeakestateorders@gmail.com.
