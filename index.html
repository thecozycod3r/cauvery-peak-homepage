III Process

Grower to connoisseur

Nine stages.
One boundary.

Every one of them happens on this plantation. That is what single estate is supposed to mean.

  1. 01

    Nursery

    Seed taken from selected mother plants and raised in the estate nursery. Three years pass before a first crop.

  2. 02

    Cultivation

    Grown under two tiers of shade inside the existing forest, alongside pepper and fruit on the same land.

  3. 03

    Harvest

    Picked by hand and selectively — only ripe cherry comes off the branch.

  4. 04

    Pulping

    Sorted and pulped the same day it comes off the tree. Nothing waits overnight in cherry.

  5. 05

    Washing

    Fermented overnight in tanks, then the mucilage is washed clean through the channels.

  6. 06

    Drying

    Sun-dried on concrete terraces, turned by hand, down to under 12% moisture.

  7. 07

    Milling & grading

    Hulled at the dry mill, then graded by size and density. Defects are taken out by hand.

  8. 08

    Roasting

    Roasted after your order is placed, in small batches, on the estate. Never before, never to sit in a warehouse.

  9. 09

    Packed & shipped

    Rested 24 hours to cool, packed, and sent from the estate itself.

V Heritage

1867 to today

Four names,
five generations,
three estates.

The estate was first assigned in 1867. The family bought Glenfell in 1937 and Cauvery Peak in 1957. In 1965 MSP Rajes travelled to Costa Rica and brought back the Hawaiian Red Caturra seed the estate still grows today.
