MSP Plantations · Yercaud, Tamil Nadu

Three places

Coffee is better at the source.

One café on the plantation itself, one on the main road into Yercaud, and a kiosk on the ghat road halfway up the hill.

The plantation one

Cauvery Peak Estate Café

A café on a 150-year-old coffee estate, in a quaint building with modern equipment, facing the coffee nursery. The drive up from Yercaud town is 15 km on a well-paved scenic road.

  • Open7.30am – 5.00pm
  • ClosedTuesdays
  • Telephone+91 75984 35955
  • Also+91 94874 58387
  • WhereMSP Plantations, on the plantation — 15 km from Yercaud town
  • Freshly brewed estate coffee
  • Retail: coffee, pepper, cloves, nutmeg, mace, cardamom, honey
  • Well-appointed rest rooms
  • Parking on site
  • Starting point for the estate tour
Get directions

The one on the way up

Lake View Village Café

On Yercaud Main Road, with ample parking and a spacious interior. The natural stop to break a drive, and the easiest of the three to reach.

  • Open8.00am – 5.00pm
  • ClosedTuesdays
  • Telephone+91 75984 35955
  • Also+91 63808 05161
  • WhereMSP Plantations, Yercaud Main Road
  • Coffee, snacks and local delicacies
  • Retail corner for beans and merchandise
  • Ample parking
  • Spacious indoor seating
  • Views over the surrounding country
Get directions

The one on the ghat

Glenfell Estate Kiosk

At the 17th hairpin bend on the Salem–Yercaud ghat road. A coffee stop with a view down the valley, and a place to stretch your legs halfway up the hill.

  • Open7.30am – 5.00pm
  • ClosedTuesdays
  • Telephone+91 75984 35955
  • Also+91 94891 80236
  • WhereMSP Plantations, 17th Hairpin Bend, Salem–Yercaud Ghat Road
  • Freshly brewed coffee to go
  • Packaged beans and ground coffee
  • Panoramic valley views
  • A well-known photo stop
  • Best at sunrise and sunset
Get directions
